Questions, answered

Good to know before a demo.

What is Garvitt?

Garvitt is an AI voice receptionist concept for HVAC businesses. It is designed to handle customer enquiries and support configured appointment workflows.

Can Garvitt book appointments?

The demonstrated workflow checks technician availability and confirms a booking using the configured backend. The exact behaviour depends on the business's schedule, rules and integration setup.

Can it reschedule or cancel appointments?

Yes. The supplied demonstration shows an existing appointment being found, moved from 8 AM to 11 AM, and then cancelled after confirmation.

Does it work outside business hours?

The AI can be configured to handle enquiries outside office hours. Actual call answering depends on the phone-line deployment and operating configuration. This demo does not establish that a live public phone line is connected.

Can it answer HVAC questions?

It can answer common questions when the relevant business information and response rules are configured. Technical or safety-critical issues should be escalated according to your business policy.

Can it work with our existing booking system?

That depends on the system and available integration methods. Each deployment needs a compatibility review and testing; compatibility with every platform is not guaranteed.

Are these live phone calls?

No. The recordings on this page are web-call demonstrations. Connecting a business telephone number is a separate deployment step.

Is the spreadsheet on this page live?

No. The screenshots are visual evidence from the supplied tests. They are shown as demonstration images and are not a live spreadsheet connection.
